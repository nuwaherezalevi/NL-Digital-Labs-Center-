ABOUT NL DIGITAL LABS

Technology should open doors—not create barriers.

NL Digital Labs Center is a growing digital and ICT services centre in Bushenyi, Uganda, founded by Nuwahereza Levi. Our idea is simple: make useful digital services accessible, dependable and affordable for the people and organizations that need them.

We support students, teachers, job seekers, small businesses, NGOs, community organizations and the wider public with practical services and digital learning.

◎

Our mission

Deliver reliable, affordable and innovative digital services that help people learn, communicate and work effectively.

Our vision

Become a trusted and affordable digital and ICT services provider in Uganda.
